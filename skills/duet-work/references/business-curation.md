# Business curation

The procedure of a routine whose `routine-type` is `business-curation`: the standing work of watching one business as a whole. Its chat is the business's Curator. This file holds the method shared by every business; the routine's own `INDEX.md` holds what belongs to its business — its purpose there, local checks, the current focus and the limits of what I have actually asked for.

## What curation is

The Curator looks at the whole business and brings me decisions: which work to close, which to open, what to move, where two works share one subject, where a work and the disk disagree. Nothing in the business is "someone else's" to the Curator: uncommitted changes in a product repository, a stale ticket, a file of unknown origin — it is the Curator's job to find out what it is and give a plan for it.

The Curator does not run the works themselves: a project's tasks live in that project's folder, not here. It names a discrepancy in the picture and fixes it on my word; what the routine's own `INDEX.md` lists as already assigned needs no second word.

The routine never completes. Finishing a cleanup, a discussion or a ticket does not close it.

## The folder

- `INDEX.md` — the handoff to the next chat: current focus, unfinished matters, whose move it is, the limits of the assignment.
- `Grand_picture.md` — the document for me: the map of the business and the questions it puts before me. A big picture belongs to one program; the grand picture covers the whole business.
- `Backlog.md` — unfinished and deferred matters of curation. It is the Curator's memory, not a queue to execute.
- `archive/` — closed one-off episodes, with a short `INDEX.md`. Their reports are history, not current instructions.

## The cycle

1. Read the composition and states of the works from the `tickets` tool. Do not copy them into files: the tool reads the folders and is always right, and a copy is stale after the first move.
2. Check where each work belongs and how works connect: to programs, to standing folders, to repositories.
3. Find the changes that need my decision: close, open, move, split.
4. Update the grand picture and the handoff.

The routine's `INDEX.md` may add steps that only its business needs.

## Starting a chat

Read the routine's `INDEX.md` first. Then open only the document the conversation needs: the grand picture to discuss the business, the backlog to choose the next matter of curation. Do not read the archive by default.

## Handing over

Before the work ends, update the focus in `INDEX.md`: what is decided, what is not, whose move is next, what is actually permitted. Update the picture in `Grand_picture.md` and the deferred in `Backlog.md`. Leave nothing of value only in the chat.

A closed one-off episode moves to `archive/` with a short `INDEX.md`: the result, the consequences left open, what the kept materials are for. Its conclusions that still hold go into the current documents first. Do not pile up reports in the root, do not keep a chronicle in place of the state, and do not create a second handoff file.
